About Us

A Focused, Reliable Wool & Sheepskin Supplier

WoolyHome develops natural wool dryer balls and sheepskin gloves for B2B buyers who need consistent quality and flexible customization.

Our Story

Natural Products, Kept Simple

WoolyHome is based in Shijiazhuang, Hebei, China. We currently focus on two natural product lines — wool dryer balls and sheepskin gloves — so every order gets full attention on material sourcing, production quality, and packaging detail.

We work with brands, wholesalers, retailers, and private label partners who want a manufacturer that is easy to reach and easy to work with, from first sample to bulk shipment.
